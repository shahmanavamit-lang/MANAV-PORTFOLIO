CONCEPTUAL FRAMEWORK / RESEARCH OVERVIEW6 May 2026

An Ethical Domain-Specific Generative AI Framework for Adolescent Mental Health Triage in India

AI ethics · Human-centred design · Mental-health triage

Read the full paper ↗View on Zenodo ↗
Shah Manav Amit · DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.20058634

A proposed framework for bounded, referral-oriented AI triage for Indian adolescents, with explicit interaction limits and pathways to human professionals.

The question

How could generative AI support access to care while keeping its role carefully bounded? This paper proposes a platform-neutral triage framework for Indian adolescents aged 16–18.

The proposed architecture

The framework combines constrained conversational scope, limited interaction cycles, risk-detection mechanisms, and referral pathways to human professionals. Its design prioritizes ending the AI interaction once a referral is appropriate.

The work considers culturally familiar expressions of distress, such as academic stress and overthinking, as part of the proposed assessment flow. The aim is to make a pathway to professional support more accessible while preventing the system from taking on a therapeutic role.

Research method and status

This is a conceptual and architectural study based on thematic literature synthesis and constraint-based modelling. It has not been empirically validated through deployment with adolescents, and it does not establish clinical effectiveness.

Next steps identified in the paper

The proposed next steps include culturally adaptive language calibration, ethically reviewed pilot studies, and practitioner-led auditing of risk detection and referral outcomes.
